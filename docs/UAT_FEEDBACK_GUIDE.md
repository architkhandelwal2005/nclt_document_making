# UAT feedback workflow

Use **Test Feedback** in the left navigation whenever the office encounters a
repeatable issue during testing.

1. Select the company only when it is needed to identify the affected workspace.
2. Enter the module, the exact steps taken, the expected result, and the actual
   result or visible error.
3. Choose severity: **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, or **Blocker**. A Blocker
   means the tester cannot continue that workflow.
4. Do not enter passwords, client documents, personal data, claim evidence, or
   screenshots containing confidential information. Send a suitably redacted
   screenshot separately if it is needed.
5. Administrators and the Insolvency Professional can triage reports as New,
   Triaged, In progress, Resolved, or Deferred and record an internal note.

Access rules:

- A tester sees only reports they created.
- An administrator or Insolvency Professional sees the firm-wide queue.
- Viewer accounts may submit a UAT report, but remain unable to alter company
  data or workflow records.
- A report with a selected company is checked against the reporter's existing
  case assignment.

The report register is operational support metadata. It does not upload or copy
case documents, and it is not a legal case-workflow record.
